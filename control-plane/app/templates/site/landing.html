Privacy by design

What happens in the room stays in the room

Here is exactly what stays on the device, and the little that doesn't.

Stays on the device

  • Video, audio and screen sharing
  • Chat messages
  • Meeting titles and participant names
  • User accounts and passwords

Sent to our management service

  • Device health: temperature, storage, uptime, software version
  • Licence status
  • The device's local network address, to give you a private office link

Compliance

Ready for regulators

PDPA & GDPR ready

Built around the principles of Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act and the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

  • Data residency. Meeting data is processed and stored on the device, on your premises.
  • Data minimisation. Only device health and licence status ever leave the building.
  • No third-party processors for video, audio, chat or participant names.
  • You stay in control. Your administrators decide who has an account.

VIPCON helps you meet your obligations under the PDPA and GDPR. Overall compliance also depends on your organisation's own policies and how the system is used.
