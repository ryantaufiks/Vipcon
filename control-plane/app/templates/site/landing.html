How it works

From the box to your first meeting in minutes

  1. 01

    Place it in the room

    Power it on and the touch screen shows your brand within seconds. Plug in Ethernet, or connect Wi-Fi from your phone by scanning the code on the screen.

  2. 02

    Activate with a scan

    Scan the activation code and create your administrator account. There's no software to install and no server to set up.

  3. 03

    Meet

    Create meetings and share links. People in the room scan the screen to see the meetings in progress and join from their browser on a laptop, tablet or phone.

Privacy by design

What happens in the room stays in the room

VIPCON is managed centrally but never routes your meetings through the cloud. Here is exactly what stays on the device, and the little that doesn't.

Stays on the device

  • Video, audio and screen sharing
  • Chat messages
  • Meeting titles and participant names
  • User accounts and passwords

Sent to the management service

  • Device health: temperature, storage, uptime, software version
  • Licence status
  • The device's local network address, to publish your private office link
  • Trusted HTTPS on your network. A short office link with a real certificate, so there's no warning to click through and nothing to install on phones.
  • Signed end to end. Every request a device makes is signed, and licences are cryptographically signed too.
  • Nothing to install. Participants join from any modern browser.
